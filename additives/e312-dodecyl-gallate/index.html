What it is

E312 (Dodecyl gallate) is a synthetic antioxidant from the gallate family, designed to extend the shelf life of fats and oils.

How it is used in food

Antioxidant.

Commonly found in
  • Edible oils and fats
  • Margarine and vegetable spreads
  • Chewing gum

Health and safety

Lacks recent safety data.

During its re-evaluation, the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) found that there was insufficient toxicological data to establish a safe Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) for dodecyl gallate. Consequently, it was removed from the list of authorised food additives.

Skin sensitisation

sensitive individuals · evidence: suspected

Gallates are recognised contact sensitisers, the same concern documented for propyl gallate (E 310). For dodecyl gallate the wider toxicological picture is unresolved rather than reassuring: EFSA found the data inadequate to confirm safety at all.

EFSA re-evaluation of dodecyl gallate (E 312)

Its EU authorization was dropped in 2018 when producers submitted no supporting data.

Conclusion

E312 is no longer legally permitted in European foods. If you see it on a label, it may be an imported product non-compliant with EU regulations, or an outdated label.
