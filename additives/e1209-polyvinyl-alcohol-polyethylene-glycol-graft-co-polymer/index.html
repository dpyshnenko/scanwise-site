E1209 · Glazing agents

Polyvinyl alcohol-polyethylene glycol-graft co-polymer

Limited risk

Provides a glossy, protective coat for nutritional supplements.

Category
Glazing agents
Vegan
Yes
Vegetarian
Yes

What it is

A highly flexible synthetic film coating used to make supplement tablets easy to swallow and visually appealing.

How it is used in food

Provides a highly flexible, glossy coating for food supplements that prevents moisture damage.

Commonly found in
  • Food supplements

Health and safety

No known risks at typical supplement intake levels.

EFSA evaluated its safety and approved it as a glazing agent for solid food supplements.

Worth knowing

It is a grafted copolymer, combining the properties of two different plastics into a safe, digestible film.

Conclusion

Provides a glossy, protective coat for nutritional supplements.
