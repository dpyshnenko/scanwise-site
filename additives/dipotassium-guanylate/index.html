E628

Dipotassium guanylate

Limited risk

Nucleotide flavor enhancer.

Vegan
maybe
Vegetarian
maybe

What it is

A flavor enhancer that provides a savory, umami taste, similar to MSG but much more potent.

How it is used in food

Allows food manufacturers to drastically reduce the salt content of a product while maintaining a strong, savory flavor.

Commonly found in
  • Processed foods

Health and safety

Generally recognized as safe (GRAS) at authorized levels.

Regularly evaluated by EFSA for safety.

Conclusion

This nucleotide flavor enhancer presents a bounded risk and is generally recognised as safe at authorised levels. By providing a potent savoury taste, it allows food manufacturers to reduce salt content in processed foods, making it a reasonable trade-off unless you consume massive amounts of heavily processed meals.
