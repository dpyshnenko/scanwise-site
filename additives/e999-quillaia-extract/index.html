What it is

Quillaia extract (E999) is a natural foaming agent and emulsifier. It is rich in saponins—natural soap-like compounds that trap air and create stable foam.

How it is used in food

Make root beer’s head of foam.

Commonly found in
  • Ginger beer and root beer (to create the foamy head)
  • Ciders and fizzy soft drinks
  • Slushies and frozen beverages

Health and safety

None major at food doses.

At food doses it just makes bubbles.

How it is made

It is extracted by milling the inner bark of the Quillaja saponaria (the soapbark tree), which is native to Chile, and steeping it in water.

  1. Extraction — The inner bark of the soapbark tree is milled and steeped in hot water, then filtered and dried.

Worth knowing

Extract of the Chilean soap-bark tree, whose natural saponins also act as a vaccine adjuvant.

Conclusion

A rare and specific additive, E999 is highly valued in the beverage industry for its ability to create a thick, appealing foam on drinks that wouldn't normally hold a head.
