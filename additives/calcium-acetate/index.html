What it is

Calcium acetate is the calcium salt of acetic acid, used to buffer acidity and firm texture.

How it is used in food

Controls acidity and firms texture while supplying calcium.

Commonly found in
  • Cheese
  • Baked goods
  • Cured meats
  • Beverages

Health and safety

None at food levels — both parts, calcium and acetate, are handled routinely by the body.

No safety concern identified; permitted at quantum satis.

Worth knowing

At medicinal doses it is prescribed to kidney patients as a phosphate binder — it locks up dietary phosphate in the gut.
