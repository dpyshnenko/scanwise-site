E1104 · Flavour enhancers

Lipase

No known risk

A safe enzyme; verify animal/microbial origin.

Category
Flavour enhancers
Vegan

What it is

An enzyme that breaks down fats, used heavily in cheese production to accelerate flavor development.

How it is used in food

Commonly found in
  • Cheese
  • Dairy flavors

Health and safety

Completely safe. Vegans should check if the lipase is derived from animal stomachs or microbial sources.
