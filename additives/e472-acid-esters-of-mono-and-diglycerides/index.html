E472 · Emulsifiers

Acid esters of mono- and diglycerides

No known risk

Safe for consumption. Often animal-derived unless certified vegan.

What it is

A broad category (including E472a-f) of synthetic fats used widely in commercial baking.

How it is used in food

Commonly found in
  • Bread
  • Cakes
  • Desserts

Health and safety

Completely safe; breaks down into common dietary fats and acids.
