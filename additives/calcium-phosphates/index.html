Calcium phosphates

What it is

A phosphate salt used as an anti-caking agent, dough conditioner, or calcium fortifier.

How it is used in food

Acts as an excellent anti-caking agent, keeping powdered foods flowing smoothly.

Commonly found in
  • Baking powder
  • Fortified cereals
  • Processed cheese

Health and safety

Calcium phosphate itself is benign — it is what your bones are built from. The concern is cumulative: phosphate additives across a whole diet add up, and that total matters for anyone with reduced kidney function, who cannot clear the excess.

Excessive Phosphorus Intake

Individuals with chronic kidney disease · evidence: suspected

High dietary intake of added phosphates can disrupt calcium balance and put stress on the kidneys over time.

EFSA set a group acceptable intake of 40 mg phosphorus per kg of body weight per day in 2019, covering all phosphate additives together — the limit is about total phosphate across the diet, not this additive alone.
