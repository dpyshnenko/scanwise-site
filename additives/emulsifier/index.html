GENERIC_EMULSIFIER

Emulsifier

Limited risk

Generic emulsifier class.

What it is

A broad, generic term used on ingredient lists when specific E-numbers are not required by local regulations.

How it is used in food

Protects the manufacturer's proprietary recipe or "trade secret" blends.

Commonly found in
  • Processed foods

Health and safety

Because this is a catch-all term, it is impossible to determine the exact safety profile without contacting the manufacturer, though all must legally be food-safe.

Regularly evaluated by EFSA for safety.

Worth knowing

In the EU, manufacturers are usually required to list the specific E-number, but in other jurisdictions like the US, generic terms like "natural flavoring" are perfectly legal and common.
